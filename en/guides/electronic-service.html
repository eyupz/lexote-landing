When are the five days for electronic service added?

UETS receipt, deemed service and the procedural deadline are separate stages.

Reviewed: 3 November 2026 · Lexote product guide

For electronic service under Turkish Law No. 7201, the deemed service date is determined first: service takes effect at the end of the fifth day following delivery to the electronic address. The delivery day is excluded from those five calendar days. The applicable procedural period is then calculated under its own rules.

Separate stages

  1. UETS receipt: 29 January 2026.
  2. Deemed service: at the end of 3 February 2026.
  3. Base result for one calendar month: 3 March 2026, calculated from 3 February.

Adding one month to 29 January and then adding five days is a different calculation. Public holidays, judicial recess and special provisions may affect the final deadline separately.

Using the upcoming Lexote update

These steps apply to the upcoming app update. The current store version may use a different screen or calculation.

Choose the duration calculator when adding a note. Enter the UETS receipt date and enable electronic service. Lexote shows the receipt date and deemed service date separately, then applies your selected period to the latter. If you already entered the deemed service date, do not add the five days again.

Review the saved date separately from informational holiday alternatives. Your selected clock time is a personal reminder time; it does not determine a court filing cutoff. Lexote does not connect to UETS or automatically read service documents.

Sources and scope

Law No. 7201, Article 7/a; Electronic Service Regulation, Article 9(6), Ministry of Justice. The linked authoritative texts are in Turkish.
