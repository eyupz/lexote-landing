Why is one month different from 30 days?

Calendar months, day counts and conditional deadline adjustments explained.

Reviewed: 3 November 2026 · Lexote product guide

Article 92 of the Turkish Code of Civil Procedure distinguishes day periods from periods stated in weeks, months or years. Replacing a month with a fixed 30 days can produce a different date.

A simple comparison

Starting date1 month30 days
15 January 202615 February 202614 February 2026
31 January 202628 February 20262 March 2026

These are calendar results before holiday adjustments. If the corresponding day is absent from the final month, the last day of that month is used.

Combined with electronic service

Receipt on 29 January 2026 produces a deemed service date of 3 February. One month from that date produces 3 March; 30 days produces 5 March. The five-day stage is evaluated before the procedural period.

Selecting a period in Lexote

Use the unit specified by the relevant document or law. Do not substitute 30 days for one month. Choosing HMK, İYUK or CMK changes the contextual holiday explanation; special provisions, the type of proceeding and exceptions still require case-specific checking.

A corrected calculation does not silently change an existing note. Review the old and new dates and explicitly apply the new date.

Source

Law No. 6100, Articles 91–93, Ministry of Justice (Turkish). Check applicability before using these examples under another procedure.
