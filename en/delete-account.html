Delete your Lexote account

You can request deletion of your Lexote account and its associated data in the app or by email.

Türkçe

Request deletion outside the app

Send your request from the email address registered to your account. Do not include your password, verification codes or case records. We may ask you to confirm ownership.

Request account deletion by email

You can also use the email link on the existing account deletion page.

Delete in the app

In Lexote, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. If you have a registered account, confirm with your password in the app. For a guest account, Settings → Account → Sign Out starts deletion of the anonymous account.

Data deleted and data that may be retained

Account deletion covers your profile, account notes, audio recordings and notification records. Backup copies are restricted from active use and removed within three months. Security logs may be retained for up to 30 days. Support and request records may be kept for the applicable legal retention period. Technical diagnostics that are not tied to your account are subject to the relevant provider’s retention terms.
