Privacy Policy and KVKK Information Notice

1. Data Controller and Scope

This notice is prepared under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law, Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”), and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform, to inform users of the Lexote mobile application for lawyers’ deadline tracking (the “Application”) and lexote.com.tr, and to set out the privacy principles.

Data controller: Eyup Zarifoğlu

Location: Onikişubat / Kahramanmaraş, Türkiye

Contact: info@lexote.com.tr

The controller’s full address is available on request at info@lexote.com.tr for applications under KVKK.

In this notice, “Lexote” means the mobile application and website provided by the controller identified above.

Lexote (Eyup Zarifoğlu) is the data controller for account data, support correspondence, technical data and crash/diagnostic data, for which it determines the processing purposes and essential means, the data collected, retention periods and recipient groups. For client or third-party data, file and court information and deadline data manually entered by users in their professional work, the user determines the purposes and means and is the data controller; Lexote acts as a processor, hosting data, calculating the final day and sending reminders solely on the user’s instructions.

The obligation to inform under KVKK Article 10 is a unilateral notice and does not depend on the user’s approval or explicit consent. This text is presented electronically when personal data is obtained. To evidence that the obligation has been fulfilled, the system records the date and timestamp when the relevant version was presented. If a processing activity requires explicit consent, that consent is obtained as a separate, optional choice, independently of any confirmation that the information notice was read. Access to basic Application services is not conditional on explicit consent.

2. Personal Data Processed and Collection Methods

Personal data is collected electronically when users manually complete registration, note/deadline and support forms in the Application, enable device permissions, and when technical records are generated automatically by server infrastructure. The categories processed are:

Account data: Name, email address, password (stored irreversibly only by Firebase Authentication and not visible to us), account creation/update times and notification preferences.

Guest use: Only an anonymous, randomly generated user identifier is created when the Application is used without registration. No name or email address is collected.

Application content and manually entered deadline data: Starting and ending dates and lengths of periods manually entered for deadline tracking, final-day information calculated from these inputs, notes, descriptions, court/institution information, file numbers, voice notes (audio recordings) and creation/update times.

Support correspondence: Your email address, message, and the Application version and device model/operating system details automatically added when you send a support email from the Application.

Technical data: Connection records (including IP addresses in server logs) and error logs automatically generated by infrastructure providers to deliver and secure the service.

Crash and diagnostic data: If the Application closes unexpectedly, a crash report is automatically generated to help resolve the error. It contains a technical error trace, Application version, device model and operating-system version. Notes, client information, audio recordings and account identifiers are not included; crash records are not linked to your account.

The Application does not use advertising identifiers, collect location data, request contacts/photo access or operate analytics for tracking or profiling users. The only measurement data collected consists of the crash reports described above.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases

Data is processed only for the following purposes and their corresponding legal bases, in accordance with KVKK Article 4: lawfulness and fairness; accuracy and currency where necessary; specified, explicit and legitimate purposes; relevance, limitation and proportionality to those purposes; and retention only as long as necessary.

• Account data: Creating accounts, authenticating users and managing sessions, where processing the parties’ personal data is necessary and directly related to the establishment or performance of a contract (KVKK Article 5(2)(c)).

• Application content and manual deadline data, including notes, court/file information, periods and audio: Storing notes and recordings, synchronising and accessing them across devices, calculating final days from manually entered dates/periods and sending reminders/notifications, where necessary to establish or perform a contract (KVKK Article 5(2)(c)). Sending device notifications, using the microphone and adding phone-calendar entries rely on device permissions freely given in device settings and explicit consent (KVKK Article 5(1)).

• Password resets, technical data and crash/diagnostic data: Processing resets, preventing unauthorised access and abuse, ensuring information security, identifying and resolving server/Application errors, where processing is necessary for the controller’s legitimate interest in system security and service continuity without harming the data subject’s fundamental rights and freedoms (KVKK Article 5(2)(f)).

• Support correspondence: Answering requests and complaints and providing technical support, on the bases of contractual performance (KVKK Article 5(2)(c)) and the controller’s legitimate interest in service quality (Article 5(2)(f)).

• Legal obligations and disputes: Responding to competent courts and administrative authorities, handling data-subject requests and providing evidence in possible disputes, where necessary to fulfil the controller’s legal obligations (KVKK Article 5(2)(ç)) or establish, exercise or protect a right (Article 5(2)(e)).

Data is not processed for unspecified purposes outside those expressly listed. It is not used for marketing, sold or transferred to third parties for advertising. Once a processing purpose ends, data is not reused for a different purpose without a new legal basis.

4. Client Data, Professional Confidentiality and Processing Conditions

Notes and deadline records may contain personal data concerning clients or third parties. Because you determine the purposes and means of processing it, you are the controller. Lexote acts as a processor, hosting data, calculating periods and generating notifications only on your electronic instructions. The parties’ rights and obligations are:

• You alone must inform clients and relevant third parties under KVKK Article 10 and obtain explicit consent where required.

• Your confidentiality duties under Article 36 of the Attorneyship Law, Law No. 1136, remain unaffected.

• Under the data-minimisation principle in KVKK Article 4, do not enter more personal data than required for deadline tracking. Mask or abbreviate unnecessary direct identifiers, such as Turkish identity numbers, and do not record special-category data under KVKK Article 6, including health or criminal-conviction data, in the Application.

• As processor, Lexote does not read or analyse your notes or use them for its own purposes. It processes them only on your in-Application commands to save, calculate, update or delete.

• As controller, you may always oversee and control entered data through the Application’s access and deletion tools. When the service relationship ends or you give a deletion command, Lexote deletes client and file data and makes it inaccessible under the periods and methods in Section 7.

5. Guest (Unregistered) Use

Without registration, your data is stored on the server under an anonymous identifier tied to your device. Therefore:

• Data can be accessed only from that device, not from another device.

• Deleting the Application or signing out of the guest session permanently removes your access.

• If you register while using guest mode, existing data is transferred to the new account.

• To delete data, use the in-Application account-deletion option before signing out. After sign-out, we may be unable to match a deletion request because the anonymous identifier is unknown; records are then deleted within the retention periods below.

6. Transfers and International Transfers

Data is not transferred to unrelated third parties. To provide the service technically, it is shared only with the following infrastructure providers (recipient groups), limited to the stated categories and purposes:

• Google (Firebase / Google Cloud): Account data, anonymous user identifiers, Application content (notes, manual periods/dates, court/file information and audio), technical data and crash/diagnostic data. Purposes: Authentication and session management (Firebase Authentication), database hosting (Cloud Firestore — Frankfurt, Germany), server functions (Belgium), audio storage (Cloud Storage), Application integrity verification (App Check) and crash reporting (Crashlytics). Google processes this data as a processor on its cloud infrastructure.

• Resend: Email addresses and one-time verification codes, solely to deliver password-reset codes by email.

• Apple (Apple Push Notification service / App Store) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging / Google Play Store): Device push tokens, notification content and technical distribution/rating records, to distribute the Application, deliver deadline reminders and provide in-store ratings.

International transfers resulting from these providers’ servers being abroad are conducted under KVKK Article 9 within standard contractual clauses and the providers’ data-protection and processing commitments, limited to the technical scope necessary to perform the service.

7. Retention and Disposal

Data is retained only as long as the processing purposes require. Under KVKK Article 7 and the Regulation on the Erasure, Destruction or Anonymisation of Personal Data, when all processing conditions cease to apply or retention expires, data is erased, destroyed or anonymised on our initiative or at your request:

• Active notes and deadline records: Retained until deleted or marked as completed by the user in the Application.

• Completed notes: Automatically moved to the in-Application trash three days after being marked as completed.

• Trash: Records are permanently deleted from the system after 30 days. Before then, you can restore them or delete them permanently immediately.

• Account deletion: When you delete your account in the Application, all associated notes, manual deadline records, audio and notification records are immediately and automatically deleted on the server so they cannot be accessed or reused by the relevant users.

• Password-reset codes: Valid for ten minutes; associated transaction records are retained briefly to prevent abuse and then deleted.

• Server logs: Retained for the infrastructure provider’s default security periods, up to 30 days, and automatically deleted at the end.

• Support correspondence and KVKK request records: Retained with restricted access for the general limitation period to resolve the request and satisfy evidentiary obligations in possible disputes, then destroyed.

After deletion, data may remain briefly in infrastructure backups, encrypted and unavailable for active use. It is completely removed after the backup cycle and periodic disposal periods, no later than three months. System commands automatically ensure deletion of data transferred to third-party infrastructure providers at those providers as well.

8. Security

The following technical and organisational measures under KVKK Article 12 prevent unlawful processing/access and protect data:

• All communications use TLS; data is encrypted at rest in the provider infrastructure.

• Database and file storage use strict, regularly tested and audited security rules/access-control matrices under segregation-of-duties principles. Users authenticate to access only their own records and cannot view other users’ content.

• Passwords are not visible to us. Password-reset codes have attempt and validity limits. Online account access includes verification controls against unauthorised third parties.

• Audio recordings are held in an isolated area accessible only to their creator.

• If personal data is unlawfully obtained by others (a breach), Lexote informs the relevant user and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board without delay and as soon as possible after learning of it. Lexote supplies necessary technical information and cooperation for users’ own processes as controllers.

No internet transmission or storage is 100% secure. Protecting your device password, screen lock and account security is your responsibility for personal-data protection.

9. Permissions and Notifications

The Application requests microphone access only for voice notes, notification permission for reminders, and calendar permission only if you enable “Add notes to phone calendar” in Settings. Permissions are optional: refusing them does not prevent basic note-taking or viewing deadlines. You may deny, change or withdraw them in device settings at any time. Reminders are primarily scheduled on your device. Operating-system restrictions, network problems, battery-saving settings, background restrictions or missing permissions may cause delayed or undelivered notifications. Uninterrupted and timely delivery cannot be guaranteed.

Phone calendar: If enabled, active note titles, final dates, court/institution details and file numbers are written as events to your phone’s calendar. Choose their appearance: “Like regular events” uses your default calendar; “With a Lexote label” uses a separate gold-coloured “Lexote” calendar created on the phone. This operation does not send that information to us. The Application reads calendar events only to recognise its own entries and changes/deletes only those entries, leaving others untouched. If your calendar syncs with an account such as iCloud or Google, events are also transferred there; people with whom you share the calendar and other apps with calendar access may see them. Turning the option off or signing out removes Lexote-created events from the phone, or the separate Lexote calendar if selected.

10. Cookies

The mobile Application does not use cookies. lexote.com.tr makes only strictly necessary technical requests to display pages and run basic functions (legitimate interest under KVKK Article 5(2)(f)). It uses no functional, performance/analytical, tracking, profiling or advertising cookies requiring explicit consent.

11. Children’s Data

The Application is intended for professional lawyers and is unsuitable for persons under 18. If we discover that data concerning a person under 18 is processed, we delete the record immediately.

12. Automated Decisions, Manual Deadline Calculations and Limitation of Liability

The Application does not perform profiling or automated decision-making that produces legal effects concerning you.

Disclaimer Concerning Deadline Calculations and Notifications: The tracking feature is an auxiliary tool: the user manually enters starting dates, period lengths and other parameters; the Application uses only these inputs to calculate a final day mathematically and send reminders. It is not integrated with official service records, UYAP or court files and provides no legal advice.

Incorrect or incomplete user inputs, public holidays, the judicial recess, administrative leave, force majeure, case-specific differences in procedural rules (HMK, İYUK, CMK, İİK and other legislation), algorithmic/software errors, and device or operating-system limitations may result in an incorrect calculated final day or late reminders.

Under Articles 34 and 134 of the Attorneyship Law, Law No. 1136, the lawyer-user alone bears the duty of care and diligence in matters handled. The User must personally and independently verify calculated dates and periods against legislation and official records. Lexote (Eyup Zarifoğlu) is not responsible for loss of rights, disciplinary liability, pecuniary or non-pecuniary damage resulting from incorrect calculations, delayed/undelivered notifications, or missed statutory or strict deadlines where the user relies solely on Application calculations. All related legal, administrative and professional responsibility rests with the User. The limitation concerning calculations and notifications does not remove Lexote’s personal-data-security obligations under KVKK Article 12.

13. Your Rights and Application Procedure

By applying to the controller under KVKK Article 11, you have the right to:

• Learn whether your personal data is processed.

• Request information about processing, if it has occurred.

• Learn its purposes and whether data is used accordingly.

• Know the third parties to whom data is transferred domestically or abroad.

• Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.

• Request erasure or destruction under the conditions in KVKK Article 7.

• Request notification of correction, erasure or destruction to third-party recipients.

• Object to a result against you produced solely by automated analysis of processed data.

• Seek compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing of personal data.

You may delete notes and your account directly in the Application at any time. Send other KVKK requests to info@lexote.com.tr or in writing to the full address available on request, under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Applying to the Data Controller. A general power of attorney suffices for representatives; a specially authorised power is not required. If the request contains information enabling identity verification, formal omissions do not by themselves justify outright rejection; we contact you to complete missing information.

Properly submitted requests are resolved free of charge, with reasons, clearly and understandably, as soon as possible and within 30 days according to their nature. Responses containing personal data are delivered through secure, traceable electronic or written channels. Requests meeting erasure/destruction conditions are fulfilled within those 30 days without waiting for periodic disposal. If a request is rejected, the response is inadequate or no response is provided in time, you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

For users accessing from the European Union, GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection may also be exercised through the same contact address.

14. Changes

This policy and notice may change with the service or relevant legislation. The current version is always published here with an update date at the top. Users are informed again within the Application when processing purposes, data categories or recipient groups change; significant changes are announced in the Application.
